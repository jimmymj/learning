The 4-Step Olympiad Solving Routine

At Science Olympiad tournaments, memorized facts without structured logic will lead students into carefully crafted distractors. Every module in this app trains students in our four-step routine:

01

Identify Core Principle

Strip away scenario decoration. Ask: "What law of nature or variable is really being tested?" (e.g., Relative Density hierarchy, Law of Conservation of Mass, or Fair Test variable isolation).

02

Eliminate Edge Cases

Discard options that violate physical laws, reverse direction vectors, confuse volume with density, or claim impossible energy flow (e.g., plants gaining food from animals).

03

Test Remaining Logic

Construct a strict causal chain: If A occurs, does B follow inevitably, or is it merely an unlinked coincidence? Trace 2nd and 3rd order impacts across system boundaries.

04

Verify with Science

Sanity-check units, boundary conditions, and control variables. Ensure only ONE independent variable was tested before finalizing the tournament answer sheet.
