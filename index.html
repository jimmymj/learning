Standards & Learning Objectives

Rigorous alignment with North Carolina Standard Course of Study & Common Core

NC.8.F.1 - NC.8.F.5

Function Foundations & Growth Rates

Define, evaluate, and compare functions algebraically, graphically, and numerically. Distinguish linear from non-linear relationships, interpret rate of change (m = Δy / Δx), and analyze quadratic and exponential behavior.

Standard NC.8.G.3

Coordinate Transformations

Describe the effect of dilations, translations, rotations, and reflections on two-dimensional figures using coordinates: (x, y) → (kx, ky) with center at (0, 0) and arbitrary centers (cx, cy).

Standard NC.8.G.4

Similarity Sequence

Understand that a two-dimensional figure is similar to another if the second can be obtained from the first by a sequence of rotations, reflections, translations, and dilations.

NC.4.OA.4

Factors, Multiples & Prime/Composite

Find all factor pairs for whole numbers 1–100; demonstrate understanding that a whole number is a multiple of each of its factors; categorize numbers as prime or composite with interactive area grids and remainder bundling.

NC.4.NF.1 • NC.4.NF.2

Fraction Equivalence & 1/2 Benchmarks

Demonstrate fraction equivalence a/b = (n × a)/(n × b) using stacked tape strips and area models. Compare two fractions with different denominators (2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 100) using benchmark reasoning against 1/2.

NC.4.NF.3 • NC.4.NF.4

Operations with Fractions & Repeated Addition

Decompose fractions into sums of unit fractions (3/8 = 1/8 + 1/8 + 1/8). Add and subtract like-denominator fractions and mixed numbers. Multiply whole numbers by unit fractions (n × 1/b) via number line jumps.
