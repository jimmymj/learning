Standards & Learning Objectives

Rigorous alignment with North Carolina Standard Course of Study & Common Core

NC.8.F.1 - NC.8.F.5

Function Foundations & Growth Rates

Define, evaluate, and compare functions algebraically, graphically, and numerically. Distinguish linear from non-linear relationships, interpret rate of change (m = Δy / Δx), and analyze quadratic and exponential behavior.

Standard NC.8.G.3

Coordinate Transformations

Describe the effect of dilations, translations, rotations, and reflections on two-dimensional figures using coordinates: (x, y) → (kx, ky) with center at (0, 0) and arbitrary centers (cx, cy).

Standard NC.8.G.4

Similarity Sequence

Understand that a two-dimensional figure is similar to another if the second can be obtained from the first by a sequence of rotations, reflections, translations, and dilations.

NC.4.OA.4

Factors, Multiples & Prime/Composite

Find all factor pairs for whole numbers 1–100; demonstrate understanding that a whole number is a multiple of each of its factors; categorize numbers as prime or composite with interactive area grids and remainder bundling.

NC.4.NF.1 • NC.4.NF.2

Fraction Equivalence & 1/2 Benchmarks

Demonstrate fraction equivalence a/b = (n × a)/(n × b) using stacked tape strips and area models. Compare two fractions with different denominators (2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 100) using benchmark reasoning against 1/2.

NC.4.NF.3 • NC.4.NF.4

Operations with Fractions & Repeated Addition

Decompose fractions into sums of unit fractions (3/8 = 1/8 + 1/8 + 1/8). Add and subtract like-denominator fractions and mixed numbers. Multiply whole numbers by unit fractions (n × 1/b) via number line jumps.

NC.4.NF.6 • NC.4.NF.7

Decimal Notation & Tenths/Hundredths

Use decimal notation for fractions with denominators 10 or 100 (4/10 = 0.4 = 40/100 = 0.40). Add decimal fractions by generating equivalent fractions. Compare two decimals to hundredths using benchmark reasoning, number lines, and dual grids.

NC.4.NBT.1 • NC.4.NBT.5 • NC.4.NBT.6

Place Value, Multi-Digit Operations & Division

Recognize 10× place-value shifts across digits. Multiply a whole number of up to four digits by a one-digit whole number using area models, partial products, and properties of operations. Find whole-number quotients and remainders using partial quotients.

NC.4.MD.1 • NC.4.MD.3 • NC.4.MD.4

Measurement, Rectilinear Area & Line Plots

Decompose composite rectilinear L-shaped shapes into non-overlapping rectangles to determine area and perimeter. Convert larger measurement units to smaller units within metric and customary systems. Analyze fractional data displayed on line plots.

NC.5.MD • NC.5.NBT • NC.5.OA (AIG Q1)

Spatial Volume, Algorithmic Cryptarithms & Thousandths

Explore 3D volume layering (V = B × h), space diagonals (d = √(l² + w² + h²)), and composite cavity subtractions. Master multi-digit cryptarithms, modular remainder interpretations, exponential powers of 10, and decimal inequalities to thousandths.

NC.5 → NC.6 SSA Acceleration

Algebraic Equations, Decimals & Proportions

Solve one-step algebraic equations on balance scales ([2³ + 4] · h = 144). Prove why trailing zeros collapse in decimal operations (0.425 × 12.8 = 5.44 L). Divide fractions using common subdivisions (9/6 ÷ 4/6 = 2 ¼ hrs), and compare ratio rates via coordinate rays (y = 2.5x).
